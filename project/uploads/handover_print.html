1. Executive Summary & High-Level Architecture

Ibtida (derived from the Arabic word Ibtidā’ meaning Beginning / Genesis) is an enterprise-grade Islamic spiritual companion, worship tracking, and digital mindfulness application. Ported with pixel-level fidelity from the Ibadah v5 design system specification, Ibtida combines classical Islamic tradition with modern mobile engineering paradigms.

Core Capabilities

  1. Real-time Astronomical Calculations: High-precision prayer times calculated via the adhan astronomical engine, supporting multiple international conventions (MWL, ISNA, Umm Al-Qura, Karachi, Egypt, etc.), madhab-specific Asr timings, and dynamic spherical trigonometry for Kaaba Qibla bearing.
  2. "Ibadah Lock" (App-Blocking & Digital Fasting): Enforces distraction-free worship through a custom native bridge (modules/expo-ibadah-native). On Android, an AccessibilityService halts distracting social/gaming apps during prayer or focus sessions; on iOS, Apple's ScreenTime (FamilyControls & ManagedSettings) framework provides hardware-level application shielding.
  3. Two-Stage Wake-Verified Prayer Alarm: Combats sleep inertia and alarm snoozing by demanding physical proof of awakening: first scanning a QR code at the user's Wudu station, followed by a second verification QR scan on the prayer mat.
  4. Digital Tasbeeh & Verified Dhikr Corpus: An interactive counter equipped with physics-based pearl bead rendering, haptic feedback profiles, session timers, and a curated library of authentic Quranic and Prophetic supplications across 8 life categories (with strict content governance forbidding unverified text).
  5. Community Circles & Collective Goals: Social accountability structures allowing users to create private or invite-only circles, share dhikr milestones, and contribute personal counts toward massive collective targets (e.g., 1 Million Salawat).
  6. Kalimat Semantic Vector Search: Neural search over the Holy Quran, Sahih/Sunan Hadith literature (graded Sahih/Hasan/Da'if), and Azkar corpus.

4. Core Architectural Subsystems

4.1. Authentication, Session Lifecycle & Onboarding Gate

The authentication layer resides in src/state/AuthContext.tsx and encapsulates all interaction with Supabase Auth:

  1. Authentication Providers Supported:
    • Standard Email & Password (signUpWithEmail, signInWithEmail)
    • Passwordless Magic Link (signInWithMagicLink)
    • Google OAuth (signInWithGoogle) via expo-auth-session and expo-web-browser
  2. Deep-Link Callback Flow:
    • Standalone scheme: ibtida://auth/callback
    • Handles both query-based PKCE exchange (?code=...) and hash-fragment session payloads (#access_token=...).
    • Automatically invokes supabase.auth.setSession or supabase.auth.exchangeCodeForSession.
  3. Background Token Refresh Management:
    • Mobile operating systems do not fire browser window focus events. An active listener on React Native's AppState ('active' vs 'background') triggers supabase.auth.startAutoRefresh() when foregrounded and supabase.auth.stopAutoRefresh() when backgrounded to conserve battery and eliminate token desynchronization.
  4. Onboarding Gate Logic in App.tsx:
    • An asynchronous check evaluates isOnboardingComplete(userId) stored in AsyncStorage (ibtida.onboarded.<userId>).
    • Navigation tree evaluation:
      • Unauthenticated \(\rightarrow\) WelcomeScreen
      • Authenticated but First Launch \(\rightarrow\) IntentionsScreen
      • Authenticated & Onboarded \(\rightarrow\) TabNavigator (Tabs)

4.2. Astronomical Prayer-Time Engine & Qibla Geometry

Located in src/lib/prayerTimes.ts and backed by src/services/prayerSettings.ts:

  1. Calculations:
    • Leverages adhan-js (CalculationMethod, Coordinates, Madhab, PrayerTimes).
    • Translates database parameters (MuslimWorldLeague, Egyptian, Karachi, UmmAlQura, Dubai, Qatar, Kuwait, MoonsightingCommittee, Singapore, NorthAmerica, Tehran, Turkey) directly into solar declination equations.
    • Computes exact moments for: Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
  2. Asr Jurisprudence (Madhab):
    • Shafi / standard: Shadow length equals object height (\(1\times\)).
    • Hanafi: Shadow length equals twice object height (\(2\times\)).
  3. Qibla Direction (Spherical Trigonometry):
    • Kaaba coordinates: Latitude \(21.422487^\circ\text{ N}\), Longitude \(39.826206^\circ\text{ E}\).
    • Great-circle forward azimuth formula: \[\theta = \text{atan2}\left(\sin(\Delta\lambda)\cos(\phi_2), \cos(\phi_1)\sin(\phi_2) - \sin(\phi_1)\cos(\phi_2)\cos(\Delta\lambda)\right)\]
    • Returns normalized heading degrees (\(0^\circ - 360^\circ\)) from true north.
  4. Dynamic Countdown Window:
    • getPrayerCountdownWindow(settings, now) automatically resolves current prayer, upcoming prayer, and exact milliseconds remaining, seamlessly bridging the midnight boundary (Isha to Fajr next day).

4.3. Motivational & Prayer Alert Notification Architecture

Located in src/services/prayerNotifications.ts:

  1. The Daily Drift Problem:
    • Islamic prayer times change by 1–2 minutes daily due to seasonal solar declination. Standard repeating alarms (e.g., daily at 05:12) drift into invalidity within days.
  2. Rolling 7-Day Window Solution:
    • Pre-computes exact dates and times for 5 prayers across a rolling 7-day window.
    • Schedules one-shot notifications with explicit DATE triggers.
    • Resynchronizes seamlessly on every app foreground transition (AppState.addEventListener('change')).
  3. iOS Notification Ceiling Management:
    • iOS enforces a hard limit of 64 pending local notifications per app.
    • Allocation: 5 prayers \(\times\) 7 days (\(= 35\)) \(+\) 3 dhikr chains \(\times\) 7 days (\(= 21\)) \(= 56\) total notifications, safely below the 64-notification threshold while leaving headroom for Wake Alarms.
  4. Rich Content Library:
    • Rotates authentic hadith supplications and Quranic verses indexed by day of week (Sun through Sat).
  5. Tap Routing:
    • Notification payloads carry metadata: { kind: 'prayer-adhan' } or { kind: 'dhikr-chain' }, triggering immediate deep-linking to prayer or tasbeeh screens.

4.4. Ibadah Lock: Native App-Blocking & Digital Fasting Subsystem

Located in src/services/ibadahLock.ts and modules/expo-ibadah-native:

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  Ibadah Lock                                      |
+-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
|                Android                  |                   iOS                   |
+-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| - Mechanism: AccessibilityService       | - Mechanism: ScreenTime / ManagedSettings|
| - Intercepts: TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED | - Intercepts: Hardware shield overlay   |

|   override logged to Supabase           |   releases FamilyControls store         |
+-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
  1. Android Engine (IbadahBlockingAccessibilityService.kt):
    • Listens for AccessibilityEvent.TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED.
    • Compares the foreground package name against the blocked set stored in IbadahBlockingPrefs.
    • If blocked, immediately launches BlockedActivity with flags FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK | FLAG_ACTIVITY_CLEAR_TOP, presenting a serene interface reminding the user of their active worship session.
    • Supports in-situ unblocking via Dhikr recitation challenge (DhikrUnlockContent.kt).
  2. iOS Engine (IbadahNativeModule.swift):
    • Interfaces directly with Apple's FamilyControls and ManagedSettings frameworks.
    • Converts serialized base64 application tokens into ApplicationToken structures.
    • Activates ManagedSettingsStore().shield.applications = Set(tokens).
    • Presents native iOS Shields without running background accessibility loops.
    • Requires Apple Family Controls Entitlement approval.
  3. Emergency Override Accountability:
    • Acknowledging genuine emergencies, users can slide to unlock during an active lock session.
    • Triggers logEmergencyOverride(userId, sessionId, reason) into Supabase emergency_overrides, creating a private, reflective accountability log visible in EmergencyHistoryScreen.

4.5. Two-Stage Wake-Verified Prayer Alarm (Wudu & Mat QR Verification)

Located in src/services/wakeAlarmScheduling.ts and src/services/wakeAlarm.ts:

  1. The Problem of Sleep Inertia:
    • Traditional alarms are dismissed reflexively in half-asleep states.
  2. Two-Stage Physical Protocol:
    • Stage 1 (Wudu Station): When the alarm rings, the user must walk to their washroom/wudu station and scan the Wudu QR tag. This cancels the immediate alarm and logs logWuduScan.
    • 10-Minute Mat Grace Window: A 10-minute countdown is scheduled. If the user returns to bed and fails to reach the prayer mat, a secondary urgent alarm fires.
    • Stage 2 (Prayer Mat): The user scans the unique QR code affixed to their prayer mat (PrayerMatTagScreen). This logs logWakeVerification, marks the prayer verified, and successfully concludes the awakening sequence.
  3. Cryptographic Tag Integrity:
    • QR tags encode unique random tokens stored in prayer_alarm_settings (wudu_token, verification_token).
    • Tokens can be regenerated at any time from PrayerMatTagScreen.
  4. Android Native Full-Screen Alarm:
    • Utilizes AlarmManager.setAlarmClock() (immune to Android Doze mode).
    • Rings through WakeAlarmRingingService and launches WakeAlarmFullScreenActivity over the keyguard/lockscreen.

4.6. Adhkar Engine, Digital Tasbeeh & Dhikr Bead Physics

Located in src/screens/adhkar/TasbeehScreen.tsx and src/state/adhkarContent.ts:

  1. Bead Geometry & Orbital Mechanics:
    • Renders 33 orbital beads on a dynamic SVG circular path with radial lighting gradients and 3D highlights on the active bead.
    • Bead position calculation: \[x = \frac{\text{size}}{2} + r \cdot \cos\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot i}{33} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \quad y = \frac{\text{size}}{2} + r \cdot \sin\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot i}{33} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\]
  2. Multi-Sensory Feedback Engine:
    • Haptic triggers: Distinct haptic bursts for single count, interval milestones (\(\times 10\), \(\times 33\)), and full goal completion.
    • Undo protection: Long-press anywhere on the screen decrements count (\(-1\)) to correct accidental touches.
  3. Per-Dhikr Atmospheric Visuals:
    • Dynamic accent palette changes ring colors, particle glows, and background tints according to the active supplication (e.g., Emerald for SubhanAllah, Gold for Alhamdulillah, Amethyst for AllahuAkbar, Sapphire for Astaghfirullah, Crimson for La ilaha illallah).
  4. Content Governance (§35 Policy):
    • Absolute prohibition against AI-generated religious scripture. All 8 categories (Morning, Evening, After Salah, Protection, Forgiveness, Gratitude, Before Sleep, Travel) contain rigorously cited texts from Sahih Al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, and Jami` at-Tirmidhi.

4.7. Community Circles, Shared Goals & Collective Impact

Located in src/services/community.ts and supabase/migrations/0006_community.sql:

  1. Circles & Membership:
    • Circles have four privacy modes: Invite only, Private, Friends, Public.
    • Distinct roles: owner (can manage settings, kick members, delete circle) and member.
    • Unique 8-character cryptographic invite codes (regenerateInviteCode) shareable via standard native share sheets.
  2. Community Goal Aggregation:
    • Goals represent collective milestones (e.g., 1,000,000 Durood Sharif).
    • When a user performs Tasbeeh linked to a community goal, updateGoalProgress executes a dual write: updating their personal goal row in adhkar_goals and upserting their participant contribution in community_goal_members.
    • The total circle progress is computed as \(\sum \text{progress}\) across all participants.

4.8. Kalimat AI Semantic Search Integration

Located in src/lib/kalimatApi.ts and src/screens/shared/SearchScreen.tsx:

  1. Neural Vector Search:
    • Queries api.kalimat.dev/api/v2 with X-Api-Key authentication.
    • Searches across three distinct corpora:
      • quran: Matches verses, surahs, and juz by semantic meaning in English or Arabic.
      • sunnah: Searches major Hadith compilations (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawood, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah) with grade filtering (Sahih, Hasan, Da'if).
      • azkar: Supplications and daily invocations.
  2. Client Performance & Debouncing:
    • 350ms input debouncing prevents API flooding.
    • Active AbortController terminates stale in-flight HTTP requests when the user continues typing.

1. profiles (0001_profiles.sql)

  • Primary key id UUID references auth.users on delete cascade.
  • Columns: display_name text, avatar_url text, updated_at timestamptz.
  • Auto-created on signup via handle_new_user PostgreSQL trigger.
  • RLS: Owner-only (auth.uid() = id).

2. prayer_logs (0002_prayers.sql)

  • Composite key (user_id, log_date, prayer_name).
  • Columns: prayer_name prayer_name enum, log_date date, done boolean, logged_at timestamptz.
  • RLS: Owner-only (auth.uid() = user_id).

3. adhan_settings (0002_prayers.sql)

  • Composite key (user_id, prayer_name).
  • Columns: enabled boolean default true.
  • RLS: Owner-only (auth.uid() = user_id).

4. adhkar_goals (0003_adhkar.sql, 0012_adhkar_community_goal_link.sql)

  • Primary key id UUID default gen_random_uuid().
  • Columns: user_id UUID, title text, target int, frequency int, range int, progress int default 0, completed_at timestamptz, community_goal_id UUID references community_goals.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

5. tasbeeh_sessions (0003_adhkar.sql)

  • Primary key id UUID.
  • Columns: user_id UUID, dhikr_key text, count int, target int, duration_seconds int, created_at timestamptz.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

6. privacy_settings & notification_settings (0004_settings.sql)

  • Keyed by (user_id, key text).
  • Columns: value text (privacy) or enabled boolean (notifications).
  • RLS: Owner-only.

7. quran_bookmarks & quran_reader_settings (0005_quran.sql)

  • Bookmarks: surah_number int, ayah_number int, note text.
  • Reader Settings: font_size text, translation_id text, arabic_visible boolean.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

8. community_circles & circle_members (0006, 0008, 0013, 0014, 0015)

  • community_circles: id UUID, name text, privacy circle_privacy enum, created_by UUID, invite_code text unique.
  • circle_members: circle_id UUID, user_id UUID, role circle_role enum ('owner', 'member'), joined_at timestamptz.
  • RLS Policies:
    • Fixed in 0013_fix_circle_rls_recursion.sql to avoid infinite recursion by using direct subqueries.
    • A user can view circles where id in (select circle_id from circle_members where user_id = auth.uid()) OR where privacy = 'Public'.
    • Members can view fellow member records within their circles.

9. community_goals & community_goal_members (0006, 0012, 0014)

  • community_goals: id UUID, name text, target bigint, unit text, ends_at timestamptz, circle_id UUID.
  • community_goal_members: Composite key (goal_id, user_id). Tracks individual progress bigint.
  • RLS: Viewable by all circle members; writes scoped to auth.uid() = user_id.

10. focus_settings & focus_sessions (0007_focus.sql, 0010_ibadah_lock.sql)

  • focus_settings: duration_index int.
  • focus_sessions: goal_id UUID references adhkar_goals, duration_seconds int, completed boolean, started_at, ended_at.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

11. prayer_calc_settings (0009_prayer_calc_settings.sql)

  • Primary key user_id UUID.
  • Columns: latitude double precision, longitude double precision, city text, country text, timezone text, calculation_method text, madhab text.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

12. blocked_apps & emergency_overrides (0010_ibadah_lock.sql)

  • blocked_apps: user_id UUID, package_name text, app_name text, platform text ('ios', 'android').
  • emergency_overrides: user_id UUID, session_id UUID, reason text, unlocked_at timestamptz.
  • RLS: Owner-only.

13. prayer_alarm_settings & wake_verifications (0011_wake_alarm.sql, 0016_two_stage_wake.sql)

  • prayer_alarm_settings: user_id UUID, prayer_name text, enabled boolean, verification_token text, wudu_token text.
  • wake_verifications: Append-only log of successful scans (user_id, prayer_name, verified_date date, verified_at timestamptz, stage text).
  • RLS: Owner-only. Append-only policy (insert allowed; update disallowed; duplicate scans ignore conflicts).

8. Custom Native Module Deep Dive (modules/expo-ibadah-native)

8.1. Android Native Implementation (Kotlin)

Registered via modules/expo-ibadah-native/android:

  1. IbadahBlockingAccessibilityService.kt:
    • Manifest config: android:accessibilityEventTypes="typeWindowStateChanged".
    • Package Queries Exemption: <queries> in AndroidManifest allows discovering launchable packages on Android 11+ (API 30+).
    • Reads blocked package list from IbadahBlockingPrefs (SharedPreferences).
    • When a blacklisted package moves to foreground, launches BlockedActivity.
  2. BlockedActivity.kt:
    • Full-screen programmatic UI in dark tones reminding user of their active prayer/focus session.
    • Embeds a Dhikr recitation challenge to unlock if urgently needed.
  3. WakeAlarmScheduler.kt & WakeAlarmRingingService.kt:
    • Invokes AlarmManager.setAlarmClock() passing a PendingIntent to WakeAlarmReceiver.
    • Receiver starts WakeAlarmRingingService as a Foreground Service with type mediaPlayback or specialUse.
    • Loops alarm audio and posts full-screen intent notification launching WakeAlarmFullScreenActivity.
  4. WakeAlarmBootReceiver.kt:
    • Listens for android.intent.action.BOOT_COMPLETED to reschedule alarms persisted in WakeAlarmPrefs.

8.2. iOS Native Implementation (Swift)

Registered via modules/expo-ibadah-native/ios:

  1. IbadahNativeModule.swift:
    • Exports native methods: isAppBlockingSupported(), pickAppsToBlock(), startBlocking(), stopBlocking().
  2. FamilyActivityPickerPresenter.swift:
    • Hosts Apple's SwiftUI FamilyActivityPicker within a native UIViewController.
    • Lets the user select apps and categories without exposing private bundle identifiers.
  3. IbadahLockSupport.swift:
    • Serializes and deserializes Apple's opaque ApplicationToken structures to base64 strings for storage in TypeScript/Postgres.
  4. ManagedSettingsStore:
    • Applies ManagedSettingsStore().shield.applications = selectedTokens.
    • Apple's operating system natively shields the target applications with an OS-level lock screen.

10. Store Compliance, Limitations & Strategic Roadmap

10.1. App Store & Google Play Policy Compliance

  1. Google Play Accessibility Service Policy:
    • Android app blocking utilizes an AccessibilityService.
    • Mandatory Action: The Google Play Store listing must include an explicit, prominent user disclosure video and privacy policy explanation declaring that the Accessibility API is used exclusively for self-imposed digital fasting and application blocking during prayer, not for disability assistance or user data collection.
  2. Apple Family Controls Entitlement:
    • iOS app blocking requires the com.apple.developer.family-controls entitlement.
    • This entitlement is not self-service. The account owner must submit a request via developer.apple.com detailing the self-restriction/wellness use case. See docs/ios-family-controls-entitlement.md for full application templates.
  3. Android Exact Alarm Permission:
    • Android 12+ requires SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM or USE_EXACT_ALARM. Must be accompanied by clear in-app justification for religious prayer times.
